NASA EarthRISE Developers Academy

NASA EarthRISE Developers Academy builds the next generation of science and technology leaders by immersing student and early career scientists in projects using NASA data to address real-world challenges. EarthRISE Developers Academy hosts 10 to 14-week projects center on developing innovative applied science solutions to help partners make more effective, efficient decisions. Through collaborative projects, professional development, and cooperation with state, local, tribal, territorial, and private sector partners, the Academy equips participants with skills to accelerate innovation and bridge Earth science with the future of space exploration.

Project Overview

The research team worked with partners from the Lake Michigan Air Directors Consortium (LADC0) on a project that aimed to assess regional air quality concerns from potential sources of NO2 pollution across Chicago and the broader Cook County, Illinois, area. Utilizing TEMPO satellite data and Open Air Chicago ground sensor data collected from September 2025 to June 2026, the team created a fused data product that estimates surface-level NO2 concentrations using TEMPO column retrievals. This dashboard visualizes spatiotemporal trends of NO2 pollution across Chicago and its community areas.
