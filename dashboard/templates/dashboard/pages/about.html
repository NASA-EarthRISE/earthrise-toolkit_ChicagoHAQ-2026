About the Dashboard

The Regional Ozone Precoursor Dashboard is a visualization tool for TEMPO satellite and Open Air Chicago ground sensor NO₂ data, developed by the EarthRISE Developers Academy.

Project Overview

This project aimed to assess regional air quality concerns from potential sources of NO2 pollution across Chicago and the broader Cook County, Illinois, region. Utilizing TEMPO satellite data and Open Air Chicago ground sensor data collected from September 4, 2025 to June 1, 2026, the team created this dashboard to visualize spatiotemporal trends of NO2 pollution across Chicago. This dashboard allows users to see hourly, daily, and monthly trends of NO2 pollution across Chicago and its community areas.
