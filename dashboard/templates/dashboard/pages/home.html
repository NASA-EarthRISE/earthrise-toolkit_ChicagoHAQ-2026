Data Sources

NASA TEMPO

Tropospheric Emissions: Monitoring of Pollution (TEMPO). Geostationary satellite providing hourly NO₂, O₃, and AOD measurements over North America.

OpenAir Chicago

Ground-based air quality monitoring network. Surface NO₂ and PM 2.5 concentrations from air quality sensors around the Cook County, Chicago region.

Fusion Layer

Combined TEMPO satellite + OpenAir Chicago ground sensor data for higher resolution air quality estimates.
